Phillip Strang

Crime Fiction Author · 18 Series · 140+ Novels

Phillip Strang is one of the most prolific crime fiction authors writing today. With 18 series spanning Britain, Australia, and America, Strang has built one of the most varied and extensive catalogues in the genre.

Born in England in the late 1940s and long based in Sydney, Australia, Strang brings a genuinely international perspective to crime fiction. His detectives range from London homicide investigators to outback trackers, from FBI agents to Scottish Highland detectives — each series grounded in a distinct location and atmosphere.

About Phillip Strang

Phillip Strang was born in England in the late 1940s. From an early age he was a voracious reader, a habit that would eventually lead to a writing career spanning decades and continents.

After graduating with a degree in electronics engineering, Strang left England in his early twenties and moved to Sydney, Australia — a move that became permanent. Australia became his home, and the Australian landscape, particularly the vast and unforgiving outback, became one of the defining backdrops of his fiction.

Strang's life was never confined to one place. The years between his arrival in Australia and his emergence as a novelist were marked by extensive travel and residence in some of the world's most turbulent regions — places that would later inform the authenticity of his thriller writing, particularly the Steve Case terrorism series.

Today Phillip Strang is one of the most prolific crime fiction authors in the English language. His 18 series and 140+ novels span three continents and encompass every major strand of the crime fiction genre — from cosy-adjacent British detective fiction to outback mysteries, from FBI procedurals to international terrorism thrillers. Each series is distinct in voice, location, and character, reflecting Strang's genuine breadth of experience and his lifelong engagement with storytelling.
