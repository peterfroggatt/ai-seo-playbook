Two engines: **(A)** restructure the site so your good posts get concentrated
internal authority and prominence, and **(B)** pull external authority (links +

actually raise rankings.

## The 5 linking rules

1. **One pillar page per cluster.** Pick (or write) the most comprehensive page
   in each group as the hub — e.g. Police Procedurals → your "definitive guide"
   page (318 impr). Every spoke links **up** to its hub; the hub links **down**
   to every spoke. This is the structure Google reads as topical authority.
2. **Homepage links to the 7 pillar pages.** The homepage is your highest-
   authority page (position 4.6). Linking it directly to each pillar passes that
   authority where you want rankings. Add a "Crime Fiction Reading Guides"
   section to the homepage.
3. **Nav: one "Reading Guides" menu → the 7 pillars.** Replace the current
   Blog/Author Guides/Fiction Lists/Crime Writing category items (which dump
   users into 1,600-post archives) with a clean dropdown of the 7 pillars. Fewer,
   stronger entry points.
4. **Spokes cross-link within their cluster** (3–5 sideways links each) using
   descriptive anchors. Keeps readers in-cluster and spreads authority evenly.
5. **Every spoke links to the matching book/series page** — and every book page
   links back to its pillar. This is the two-way funnel the blog was built for.

  guide") so the funnel runs both directions.

## Where the equity comes from (power pages)

Concentrate links **out** of your strongest pages onto the priority targets:
- **Homepage** (pos 4.6) → 7 pillars + top author-funnel pages.
- **Phillip Strang Books hub** → pillars + book pages.
- **High-impression posts** (plot-twist 1,122; legal-thriller 774; priest-
  protagonists 187) → link down to weaker siblings that need a lift.

# B. Build external authority (backlinks + E-E-A-T)

Your pages rank at position 30–65 with decent content because the **domain has
little authority**. This is the real ceiling, and the least-done work. Your
advantage: you are a **real, published crime novelist** — an E-E-A-T asset the
AI-listicle farms and aggregators can never match.

## Foundation first — E-E-A-T (do before outreach)

- **Author bio block on every list/guide page:** photo, one line of credentials
  ("Phillip Strang, author of 40+ crime novels across 15 series"), link to
  About. Turns anonymous listicles into *expert* recommendations — what Google
  and AI Overviews reward.
- **Strong About/author page** with bibliography, press, and real author
  signals. Link it from every post's byline.
- **Author schema** (`Person` + `sameAs` to Amazon author page, Goodreads,
  BookBub, Wikipedia if present) sitewide — establishes the entity.

## Link acquisition — highest ROI first (author-specific)

1. **Author profiles & platforms (fast, free):** Amazon Author Central,
   Goodreads author program, BookBub, LibraryThing, Fantastic Fiction author
   listing, Books2Read. Consistent NAP + link back. Foundational.
2. **Podcast & interview circuit:** crime-fiction, indie-author, and writing
   podcasts love guests with 40+ books. Each interview = a backlink + referral
   audience + E-E-A-T. Highest ROI for a prolific author.
3. **Guest posts / contributed articles** on writing and book blogs, using your
   craft content as the hook ("How to write a plot twist" → guest version linking
   home). Your Craft pillar is the asset here.
4. **Book-blogger & reviewer outreach:** send review copies; reviews link to your
   book pages and build genuine, topical links.
5. **HARO / journalist queries (Connectively, Featured, Qwoted):** answer queries
   on crime fiction, publishing, and writing craft → links from news/media sites,
   the strongest kind.
6. **Digital PR / linkable assets:** turn your data into a story — e.g. "The most
   common settings in bestselling crime fiction 2026," "Crime-fiction reading
   trends." Pitch to book-media; these earn links at scale.
7. **Reclaim unlinked mentions:** search for existing mentions of your name/books
   that don't link, and request the link. Quick wins.

## Cadence & sequencing

1. **Weeks 1–2:** E-E-A-T foundation (bio blocks, author schema, profiles). Cheap,
   sitewide, do first.
2. **Weeks 2–8:** internal-link restructure (pillars, nav, homepage, funnels) — no
   external dependency, fully in your control, lifts rankings on its own.
3. **Ongoing:** 1–2 outreach actions/week (a podcast, a guest post, a HARO answer,
   a reviewer). Links compound; consistency beats bursts.

## How to measure it's working
- **GSC average position** on the 7 pillar clusters trending up.
- **Referring domains** growing (track monthly).
- **Clicks to book pages** rising (the funnel converting) — currently 7; that's
  the number to grow.
- Author-funnel pages ("authors like X") sending on-site clicks to series pages.

---

## Bottom line
Internal linking makes your *existing* authority land on the pages you want to
rank; backlinks + E-E-A-T *raise the total authority* available. Do the internal
restructure first (free, fast, in your control), stand up the E-E-A-T foundation
alongside it, then run steady outreach. The "authors like [X]" cluster is where
ranking strength and reader-conversion already overlap — make it the flagship.
