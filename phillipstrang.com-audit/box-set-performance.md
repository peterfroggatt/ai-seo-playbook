## What the data changed vs my earlier structural rules

1. **Not all 3-book sub-trilogies are dead.** *Cook 1–3* pulls **16,649 KENP (all US)** and *Cook 7–9* **7,170 (all US)** — US readers grab these as entry points. Keep the Cook sub-sets; don't blanket-drop 3-book sets.
2. **Low KENP ≠ drop when the set is NEW.** *Alex Harlan 7–12* (0), *Sarah Lynch 13–18* (1,590, rel Apr-26), *Maya 13–18*, *Freya 7–12* are just-released — they need time, not deletion. Give them placement; judge in 3 months.
3. **Keep BOTH Tremayne bundles.** *Complete 1–10* (85.6k) AND *1–6* (50.5k) both perform — readers use both, so don't cut the 1–6.
