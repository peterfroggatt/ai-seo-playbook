The Detective Jordan Cross Thriller Series

For fans of Tana French, Michael Connelly & Mare of Easttown

DETECTIVE JORDAN CROSS DOESN'T SOLVE COLD CASES. SHE EXPOSES THE CONSPIRACIES THAT CREATED THEM.

In the ruins of Detroit, where abandoned buildings outnumber people and institutional corruption runs fifty years deep, one detective refuses to let the forgotten stay buried.

Jordan Cross works the cases nobody wants—decades-old murders in a basement cold case unit where files gather dust and victims' families have stopped hoping for justice. She's cynical, damaged, relentless. A divorced cop who chain-smokes through sixteen-hour days because the work is all she has.

Then she finds the pattern.

Three murdered teenagers in 1989. Eight union organizers dead between 1972 and 1979. Five autoworkers ruled suicides during the 2008 financial crisis. All dismissed. All covered up. All connected to a fifty-year conspiracy of corporate murder and police corruption that reaches from factory floors to federal courthouses.

Including the "suicide" of her own father.

Fighting institutional rot destroys the people who fight it. Jordan exposes deputy chiefs, convicts executives, proves her father was murdered. She delivers justice to families who waited decades for the truth. But justice has a price. And Jordan pays it with everything she has.
